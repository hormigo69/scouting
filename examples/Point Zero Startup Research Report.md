## 1\. Executive Summary

Point Zero is an Italian deep-tech startup focused on **zero-emissions braking technology** for vehicles. Its flagship innovation is the **Zero-Emissions Driving System (ZEDS)** – an integrated in-wheel electric motor combined with a *magnetorheological* (MR) brake – designed to eliminate particulate pollution from brake wear[\[1\]](https://www.i3p.it/en/news/premio-2031-innovative-startup-point-zero-wins-primo-round-award#:~:text=The%20Primo%20Round%20award%20was,free%20mobility). By replacing conventional friction brakes with an electromagnetic MR fluid system, ZEDS aims to **revolutionize automotive braking** and dramatically reduce vehicles’ non-exhaust emissions, improving sustainability without compromising safety[\[1\]](https://www.i3p.it/en/news/premio-2031-innovative-startup-point-zero-wins-primo-round-award#:~:text=The%20Primo%20Round%20award%20was,free%20mobility). In essence, Point Zero’s mission is to make electric vehicles truly “zero-emission” by tackling brake dust and other secondary pollutants, thereby reducing the holistic carbon footprint of cars[\[2\]](https://www.point-zero.it/#:~:text=A%20zero). Founded as a Politecnico di Torino spin-off, the company has gained recognition for its novel approach to cleaner braking and its potential to meet upcoming stringent emission standards for road vehicles[\[3\]](https://tech.slashdot.org/story/25/05/21/211204/brembos-new-brakes-cut-particulate-emissions-by-90-percent#:~:text=after%20all,improving%20durability%20in%20the%20process)[\[1\]](https://www.i3p.it/en/news/premio-2031-innovative-startup-point-zero-wins-primo-round-award#:~:text=The%20Primo%20Round%20award%20was,free%20mobility).

## 2\. Detailed Description

**Mission:** Point Zero’s core mission is to *“break through”* to a cleaner future in mobility by removing pollution from braking. The company explicitly asks, *“Are electric vehicles really zero-emissions?”*, highlighting that even EVs produce secondary particulate emissions from brakes[\[4\]](https://www.point-zero.it/team/#:~:text=In%202021%20the%20team%20started,tougher%20everyday%3A%20Reduce%20Vehicle%20Emissions). Point Zero’s goal is to **redefine vehicle sustainability** by developing technology that **avoids any brake-induced pollutants**, thus *“significantly reduce the holistic carbon footprint of cars”*[\[2\]](https://www.point-zero.it/#:~:text=A%20zero). In short, the mission is to make mobility safer and greener by eliminating brake dust at the source.

**Origin:** The startup was born out of academic research at Politecnico di Torino. Point Zero is a official **spin-off of Politecnico di Torino**, originating from the university’s Innovative Electric and Hybrid Vehicles research group (Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering)[\[5\]](https://www.point-zero.it/team/#:~:text=Point%20Zero%20is%20a%20startup,department%20of%20Politecnico%20di%20Torino). The founding team – three researcher-engineers – began collaborating in 2021 with the shared vision of solving the emerging problem of brake emissions in EVs[\[4\]](https://www.point-zero.it/team/#:~:text=In%202021%20the%20team%20started,tougher%20everyday%3A%20Reduce%20Vehicle%20Emissions). The idea for ZEDS was first conceived by co-founder Giovanni Imberti during his Master’s in Automotive Engineering at Politecnico; he pitched the concept to his professors, and together with two colleagues, transformed it into a startup with the support of the university incubator[\[6\]](https://www.i3p.it/en/news/forbes-under-30-2025-eleven-startups-i3p-chosen-among-italy-100-number-ones#:~:text=The%20path%20as%20a%20startupper,of%20which%20the%20incubator%20is)[\[7\]](https://www.i3p.it/en/news/premio-2031-innovative-startup-point-zero-wins-primo-round-award#:~:text=Point%20Zero%20is%20a%20spin,incubator%20is%20a%20technical%20partner). Thus, Point Zero’s roots are in advanced automotive R\&D, and it continues to maintain close ties to Politecnico’s research ecosystem.

**Technology & Differentiation:** Point Zero’s key innovation is its **Zero-Emissions Driving System (ZEDS)** – a **braking system with no friction pads**, based on *magnetorheological fluid*. In ZEDS, an in-wheel electric drive motor is combined with an MR brake in a single unit[\[8\]](https://www.point-zero.it/technology/#:~:text=Point%20Zero%20is%20proud%20to,both%20in%20traction%20and%20braking). During braking, the MR fluid’s viscosity is altered by a magnetic field, creating a resisting torque without any physical pad-disc contact[\[8\]](https://www.point-zero.it/technology/#:~:text=Point%20Zero%20is%20proud%20to,both%20in%20traction%20and%20braking)[\[9\]](https://www.point-zero.it/technology/#:~:text=Magneto). This design prevents the creation of brake dust particles entirely, unlike conventional disc or drum brakes[\[10\]](https://www.point-zero.it/technology/#:~:text=Zero%20Emissions). According to the company, ZEDS can perform all required traction and braking maneuvers, effectively **replacing both the traditional drive motor and friction brake** in a wheel[\[1\]](https://www.i3p.it/en/news/premio-2031-innovative-startup-point-zero-wins-primo-round-award#:~:text=The%20Primo%20Round%20award%20was,free%20mobility). The system has several distinctive technical features: it maximizes regenerative braking via the motor and uses the MR brake only when additional stopping power is needed, thereby **optimizing energy efficiency while ensuring full braking performance**[\[11\]](https://www.point-zero.it/safest/#:~:text=SAFEST%20%E2%80%93%20Strategia%20per%20l%E2%80%99Amministrazione,or%20the%20battery%20or%20motor). The patented design and control algorithms manage the interplay between motor and brake to maintain safety and braking feel[\[12\]](https://www.point-zero.it/safest/#:~:text=integrated%20with%20an%20electro,or%20the%20battery%20or%20motor). Importantly, Point Zero’s MR brake can achieve peak braking torque levels comparable to standard automotive brakes, as demonstrated in feasibility studies[\[13\]](https://www.startcup-piemonte-vda.it/assets/edizioni/2024/point-zero_scheda-progetto_sc2024.pdf#:~:text=combinazione%20del%20freno%20con%20il,veicolo%20per%20il%20blocco%20freno). In other words, the system meets normal performance and safety requirements, but with the **added benefits of zero particulate emissions and an “by-wire” electronic control**. The company holds multiple patents related to this technology – including a patent for the integrated motor-MR brake system (ZEDS) and a patent for the electronic control method for electro-actuated brakes[\[14\]](https://www.point-zero.it/team/#:~:text=Zero)[\[15\]](https://www.point-zero.it/team/#:~:text=Electro,control%20method). These innovations position Point Zero as a pioneer in “emission-free” braking, differentiating it from conventional brake manufacturers by its unique combination of **clean technology and integrated vehicle dynamics control**.

## 5\. Main Competitors

Point Zero operates in the niche sector of **automotive brake innovation for emission reduction**. Its direct competitors are those developing technologies to eliminate or reduce brake particulate emissions, whether through alternative braking methods or filtration systems. The **two primary competitors** (or comparable solutions) in this specific space are:

* **Tallano Technologies (France):** Tallano is a cleantech startup pioneering a brake emission capture system called **TAMIC**. Unlike Point Zero’s approach of eliminating friction entirely, Tallano’s TAMIC attaches to conventional brakes and **vacuums up brake dust** at the source. The system uses suction and filtration to capture fine particles during braking, reportedly reducing brake particulate emissions by **85–90%**[\[32\]](https://thebrakereport.com/tallano-technologie-raises-funds-for-brake-emission-system/#:~:text=BOULOGNE%20BILLANCOURT%2C%20ILE,the%20road%20and%20rail%20sector). Founded in 2012, Tallano has a head start; it holds \~25 patents and has tested TAMIC with rail operators and automakers in Europe[\[32\]](https://thebrakereport.com/tallano-technologie-raises-funds-for-brake-emission-system/#:~:text=BOULOGNE%20BILLANCOURT%2C%20ILE,the%20road%20and%20rail%20sector)[\[33\]](https://thebrakereport.com/tallano-technologie-raises-funds-for-brake-emission-system/#:~:text=With%20a%20portfolio%20of%2025,of%20road%20or%20rail%20vehicles). Tallano raised €17.7 million in 2021 to commercialize this technology[\[34\]](https://thebrakereport.com/tallano-technologie-raises-funds-for-brake-emission-system/#:~:text=Building%20on%20this%20first%20phase,and%20several%20European%20family%20offices). As a result, Tallano is a key competitor, targeting the same problem (brake pollution) but with a retrofit/capture solution rather than a new type of brake. Both Point Zero and Tallano aim to meet upcoming Euro 7 regulations on brake dust, but their methods differ (capture vs elimination).

* **Brembo (Greentelligent Braking Solutions):** Although not a startup, Brembo – the global brake manufacturer – is directly addressing brake emissions and therefore represents competition from industry incumbents. In 2025 Brembo unveiled **Greentell** (Green Intelligent Brake), a new brake disc and pad combination that **cuts brake dust emissions by 90%**[\[3\]](https://tech.slashdot.org/story/25/05/21/211204/brembos-new-brakes-cut-particulate-emissions-by-90-percent#:~:text=after%20all,improving%20durability%20in%20the%20process). Brembo’s approach involves special coatings (using laser-deposited material on discs) and customized pad compounds to drastically reduce particulate generation[\[35\]](https://tech.slashdot.org/story/25/05/21/211204/brembos-new-brakes-cut-particulate-emissions-by-90-percent#:~:text=,improving%20durability%20in%20the%20process). This solution is designed to comply with Euro 7 standards and is being offered to OEMs for new EV models[\[3\]](https://tech.slashdot.org/story/25/05/21/211204/brembos-new-brakes-cut-particulate-emissions-by-90-percent#:~:text=after%20all,improving%20durability%20in%20the%20process). Brembo’s efforts indicate that major suppliers are actively competing in the “low/zero emission brake” space. As a well-funded incumbent, Brembo’s emerging technologies (as well as similar low-dust brake products from competitors like Continental or Bosch) form strong competition for Point Zero’s novel system. Automakers might opt for Brembo’s low-dust *friction* brakes as a simpler adoption path, which is a market challenge Point Zero will have to overcome with superior performance or cost benefits.

Other notable mentions in this sector include startups and projects focusing on **brake dust filtration or alternative braking**: e.g., Mann+Hummel (filter devices for brake calipers), Continental’s enclosed **New Wheel Concept** (which encloses brakes to contain dust), and efforts to maximize **regenerative braking** in EVs (reducing use of friction brakes). However, **direct peers** offering a new **“emission-free brake system”** are rare. Point Zero’s most direct peer is arguably Tallano (targeting the same pain point with a different tech), whereas Brembo and others are competitors in the broader sense of providing compliance solutions for brake emissions.

*(It’s worth noting that regenerative braking systems in electric vehicles are complementary but not true competitors – they reduce brake use but do not fully eliminate the need for friction brakes and thus do not completely solve brake particulate emissions[\[36\]](https://tech.slashdot.org/story/25/05/21/211204/brembos-new-brakes-cut-particulate-emissions-by-90-percent#:~:text=focusing%20on%20other%20vehicular%20sources,achieve%20that%20target%2C%20Brembo%20has). Point Zero’s system is unique in that it aims to entirely replace friction brakes.)*

## 6\. Competitive Advantage

Point Zero’s competitive edge lies in the **technical strengths of its ZEDS technology** and the comprehensive benefits it offers over both traditional brakes and other solutions. The key advantages of its magnetorheological brake system include:

* **Zero Particulate Emissions:** By design, ZEDS produces **no brake dust** or particulate matter[\[10\]](https://www.point-zero.it/technology/#:~:text=Zero%20Emissions). Since braking force is generated via magnetic fluid rather than friction, there is no wear of pads or discs. This gives Point Zero a **unique environmental benefit**, as it can meet and exceed upcoming emission regulations without add-on filters. Competing solutions that still involve friction (even with filters or coatings) can’t claim absolute zero emissions, whereas ZEDS fundamentally **eliminates the pollutant source**[\[10\]](https://www.point-zero.it/technology/#:~:text=Zero%20Emissions).

* **Reduced Maintenance & Longer Lifetime:** The MR braking system can potentially last for the vehicle’s lifetime without routine pad or disc changes[\[37\]](https://www.point-zero.it/technology/#:~:text=Reduced%20maintenance). The magnetorheological fluid and electromagnetic components suffer minimal wear compared to pads that grind down. According to Point Zero, ZEDS **“can last as long as the vehicle”** since the MR fluid’s usable life is very long[\[37\]](https://www.point-zero.it/technology/#:~:text=Reduced%20maintenance). This implies lower maintenance costs and less downtime, a selling point for fleet operators and consumers alike. (In contrast, filters or traditional brakes still require periodic service or replacement.)

* **Faster Response Time:** ZEDS is an **electro-actuated system**, which responds quicker than hydraulic brake systems. The company claims it can begin generating braking force **three times faster** than a standard hydraulic brake can react[\[38\]](https://www.point-zero.it/technology/#:~:text=Faster%20response%20time). Without the delays of pressure build-up in brake lines and pad engagement, the MR brake’s torque can be applied almost instantaneously by changing the magnetic field. This fast response improves safety (shorter reaction distance) and enables new **control algorithms** (see next point).

* **Superior Controllability (By-Wire Control):** The brake is inherently a **“brake-by-wire”** system – electronically controlled with no direct mechanical link. This means it’s highly controllable and can be finely modulated by software. Point Zero highlights that ZEDS is an ideal platform for **advanced driver-assistance systems (ADAS) and autonomous driving**, enabling precise and independent wheel control[\[39\]](https://www.point-zero.it/technology/#:~:text=Better%20controllability). The seamless integration of traction and braking in one unit allows for smoother torque management and stability control. Competing conventional brakes cannot match this level of integrated control without additional complex electronics. This could give ZEDS-equipped vehicles an edge in performance and safety features.

* **Leaner, Integrated Design:** Combining the motor and brake in the wheel hub leads to a **modular, simplified design**[\[40\]](https://www.point-zero.it/technology/#:~:text=Leaner%20design). Fewer parts (no calipers, rotors, pads, brake boosters, etc.) mean potentially lower assembly complexity and weight savings in some areas. Point Zero notes that having all components in one hub unit can **save engineering and assembly time** for manufacturers[\[40\]](https://www.point-zero.it/technology/#:~:text=Leaner%20design). In essence, ZEDS could simplify the bill-of-materials for braking and powertrain. This integration is a differentiator from solutions that bolt on to existing brake systems.

* **Energy Efficiency:** ZEDS maximizes regenerative braking usage and only uses the MR brake when necessary, ensuring that the **battery recovers as much energy as possible** during deceleration[\[12\]](https://www.point-zero.it/safest/#:~:text=integrated%20with%20an%20electro,or%20the%20battery%20or%20motor). It intelligently balances motor regen and electromagnetic braking, optimizing energy flow between battery, motor, and brake. This improves overall vehicle efficiency (extending EV range) compared to friction brakes which dissipate all braking energy as heat. The **SAFEST** control strategy developed by Point Zero is specifically about managing this efficient blend of regenerative and MR braking for each situation[\[12\]](https://www.point-zero.it/safest/#:~:text=integrated%20with%20an%20electro,or%20the%20battery%20or%20motor)[\[41\]](https://www.point-zero.it/safest/#:~:text=Efficiency%20is%20the%20key%20of,situation%2C%20combining%20safety%20and%20efficiency). Thus, the system not only avoids emissions but also contributes to better energy economy.

In summary, Point Zero’s competitive advantage is the **holistic improvement** ZEDS provides: it *simultaneously* addresses environmental impact, maintenance cost, vehicle performance, and systems integration. While competitors might achieve one or two of these benefits (e.g., low dust, or long life, or fast response), Point Zero’s technology aims to deliver **all of them in one solution**. This multi-faceted edge – if the company can successfully industrialize it – positions ZEDS as a potentially disruptive braking system for next-generation electric and hybrid vehicles.

* **Giovanni Imberti (Co-founder & CEO)** – *Automotive Engineer.* Giovanni, 28, developed the concept for Point Zero during his graduate studies. He holds a Master’s degree in Automotive Engineering from Politecnico di Torino[\[6\]](https://www.i3p.it/en/news/forbes-under-30-2025-eleven-startups-i3p-chosen-among-italy-100-number-ones#:~:text=The%20path%20as%20a%20startupper,of%20which%20the%20incubator%20is). His passion for cars and concern for environmental issues drove him to investigate how to reduce emissions from braking systems[\[6\]](https://www.i3p.it/en/news/forbes-under-30-2025-eleven-startups-i3p-chosen-among-italy-100-number-ones#:~:text=The%20path%20as%20a%20startupper,of%20which%20the%20incubator%20is). In 2021, he posed the question about EV brake emissions and formulated the early idea of an MR fluid brake. He then spearheaded the effort to turn this idea into a company, pitching it to professors and incubator mentors[\[42\]](https://www.i3p.it/en/news/forbes-under-30-2025-eleven-startups-i3p-chosen-among-italy-100-number-ones#:~:text=how%20to%20analyze%20and%20reduce,incubator%20is%20a%20technical%20partner). Under his leadership, Point Zero obtained support from the university incubator I3P and tech-transfer programs[\[42\]](https://www.i3p.it/en/news/forbes-under-30-2025-eleven-startups-i3p-chosen-among-italy-100-number-ones#:~:text=how%20to%20analyze%20and%20reduce,incubator%20is%20a%20technical%20partner). Giovanni has also co-authored multiple research papers on regenerative braking and magnetorheological brakes[\[43\]](https://www.point-zero.it/team/#:~:text=Pre,Massimiliana%20Carello%20Read%20more). As the CEO (and likely the “face” of the startup), he was present at competitions – for example, he presented ZEDS at the Premio 2031 finals in Milan in 2024[\[1\]](https://www.i3p.it/en/news/premio-2031-innovative-startup-point-zero-wins-primo-round-award#:~:text=The%20Primo%20Round%20award%20was,free%20mobility). His background is primarily academic; Point Zero is his first startup venture, arising directly from his master’s thesis project and research (which was recognized among Forbes Italy’s “100 Number Ones under 30” in 2025\)[\[6\]](https://www.i3p.it/en/news/forbes-under-30-2025-eleven-startups-i3p-chosen-among-italy-100-number-ones#:~:text=The%20path%20as%20a%20startupper,of%20which%20the%20incubator%20is)[\[25\]](https://www.i3p.it/en/news/forbes-under-30-2025-eleven-startups-i3p-chosen-among-italy-100-number-ones#:~:text=The%20young%20company%2C%20a%20spin,ups).

His doctoral research focused on electric vehicle dynamics – specifically all-wheel drive EV modeling and validation – which is closely related to powertrain and regenerative braking control[\[44\]](https://www.polito.it/didattica/dottorato-di-ricerca-e-scuola-di-specializzazione/alumni?p=038258#:~:text=Henrique%20De%20Carvalho%20Pinheiro%20,and%20validation%20to%20the). After his Ph.D., Henrique became a research associate (Assistant Professor) at Politecnico[\[45\]](https://www.polito.it/personale?p=henrique.decarvalho#:~:text=Henrique%20De%20Carvalho%20Pinheiro%20%C2%B7,DIMEAS%29%20%C2%B7%20Profilo), indicating a strong academic career. All evidence indicates a **highly qualified founding team** with a clear passion for sustainable mobility and the technical skills to realize an ambitious engineering solution. This strong team foundation, combined with the support of Politecnico’s ecosystem, bodes well for Point Zero’s ongoing trajectory as they aim to bring their zero-emission brake system to the automotive market.
